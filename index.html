About Heart Magnetica

The heart generates the body's most powerful electromagnetic field, one that extends beyond the physical body and shapes how we connect with the world and people around us. When we are disconnected from our hearts, it can create imbalance in our lives.

Many of us move through life guided by conditioning, from our thoughts, families, or society, rather than our own inner wisdom. Heart Magnetica offers a path back to a more heart-centered way of living.

By connecting with the heart's energetic field, there is greater opportunity for self-awareness, self realization and reconnection with your body's natural rhythms.

Leading a heart led life takes time.

This method isn't about quick fixes, but rather a dedication to the journey by getting to the root cause of what may be standing in your way such as childhood traumas, past lives, and any sort of unhelpful patterns.

Marlyn weaves many of the techniques she has learned over the years into this practice along with what is unique to her which is her specialty of working with the magnetism of the heart.

About Marlyn

Marlyn is a Thai American currently living in Chiang Mai, Thailand since 2022.

Her awakening journey started in 2015 as a result of searching for answers on how to heal from chronic illness. She turned to holistic healing methods when western medicine wasn't working for her.

Hungry for knowledge on how to heal naturally, Marlyn dove into the esoteric and occult worlds for 12 years learning several healing modalities with different teachers. With each new training, her body would heal miraculously more and more. Her life went from scarcity to abundance.

After 10 years of facilitating multiple modalities, Marlyn had a spontaneous heart activation while in a deep meditative state. It was something she had never experienced before in any of the lineages she had learned or facilitated. She saw how powerful the heart's electromagnetic field is and was guided to work with it, thus Heart Magnetica was born.

Marlyn is dedicated to teaching others how to lead heart led lives with an emphasis on grounding into embodiment. A reminder that it's never too late to follow your heart. She has a firm belief that changing your life is possible with time, dedication, trust, and love.

Her own heart led life is a testament to what can happen when you have the courage to find the right solutions for yourself. She has seen how working with this energy can be incredibly transformative.

The heart truly is the portal to living an abundant life.
